Privacy & security

Your university data stays yours.

What the app keeps, where it keeps it, and what it never sends.

  • University passwords stay on your phone

    SIS and LMS passwords are never sent to Firebase or to any server of ours. If you turn on Remember Me, they are kept in Android’s encrypted storage.

  • Straight from the university

    Your marks and courses travel from the university systems to the app directly. They do not pass through a server of ours.

  • Read-only by design

    It cannot register, pay, withdraw or appeal. Anything that changes your record opens the official site instead.

  • Google sign-in is optional

    Use the app as a guest if you like. Signing in with Google only links your AOU Hub account, never your university login. No ads, no ad tracking.
